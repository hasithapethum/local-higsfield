# What "beats Higgsfield" means for H3 Studio

Written 2026-11-13. Scope: video character swap (put a reference character into an existing video, keep motion, scene and audio).
Everything marked "(inferred)" is the analyst's own reasoning, not a published fact. Competitor numbers come from vendor pages or
third-party write-ups; where sources disagree, both figures are shown. Vendor pages change often, so re-check before quoting a price.

## 1. Executive summary

1. Higgsfield is a cloud front end over several models, not one swap model. Recast is the swap tool, Genjutsu is the "rebuild part of a clip" tool
   (Motion Transfer and Object Swap), LipSync Studio and Soul ID cover talking heads and identity. Its pages publish almost no quality numbers, no audio
   policy for Recast or Genjutsu, and no identity metrics. (Recast page, Genjutsu page, below.)
2. The nearest direct competitor is Viggle Remake, which is built on the very same base model as H3 Studio (MiniMax-H3 with swap/edit LoRAs). So the
   model is not the moat; verification, preparation of inputs, long-clip handling and honesty about failures are.
3. The 2026-11-13 incident (job f0eaf24e, "done" but the original person unchanged) is a failure no competitor documents but every user would judge
   as the worst possible outcome. It was measured offline for this report: on 8 frames of that output, the face embedding of the output matches the
   SOURCE person at cosine 0.77 to 0.98 (median about 0.93) and the reference character at only -0.01 to 0.07, with whole-frame SSIM 0.93 to 0.96
   against the source. That is a clean, cheap, CPU-only signature of "nothing was swapped" (section 4, test T1).
4. "Beats Higgsfield" is defined below as 10 measurable targets. H3 Studio can realistically win on: never failing silently, exact length and audio,
   per-clip cost, clip length without a cap, user control (area selection, multi-person, previews), transparency (a scored report per render).
   It cannot win today on: speed (measured 96 s of wall clock per output second on the shared GPU), output resolution (default 0.6 MP, about 480p),
   or polish and onboarding. The gap list in section 5 is ordered by impact on user trust first.

### 2.3 Known weaknesses from reviews and docs (use these as the failure list for tests)

Higgsfield's own guide, which doubles as its list of failure modes (https://higgsfield.ai/blog/AI-Face-Character-Swap-in-Video-Photo-PRO-Guide):
several faces means the closest is swapped; pure white background weakens blending and shadows; held props (cups, phones, bags) are read as body geometry and appear in the output;
mismatched colour grade between reference and footage misaligns skin texture; side angles and fast motion degrade face mapping; an occluded or contorted face makes the swap fail;
very different body builds distort. Third-party hands-on reviews of Higgsfield in general report face drift on 90-degree profiles, more drift with unusual hair, glasses or heavy makeup,
identity drift in longer clips, mixed lip-sync (one scored it 5 out of 10), and credits burned on regenerations
(https://www.g2.com/products/higgsfield/reviews , https://ai-solutes.com/higgsfield-ai-review/). These are blogs and affiliate-style reviews, so treat as anecdotal; no Recast-specific user review corpus was readable.

Other products:
- Kling: the largest person is picked, similar-sized people select nobody, cuts truncate the clip, a face that is too different from the first frame degrades (https://kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide).
- Wan-Animate: 2D skeleton ambiguity, wrong keypoints, large body-shape differences deform the result in replacement mode (https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14055v1).
- Viggle Remake: non-humanoid characters in long takes, fine-detail artifacts, timing drift in multi-shot clips rendered without pins (https://hackernoon.com/inside-viggle-remake-lora-based-video-editing-with-minimax-h3).
- DomoAI: pose and framing mismatch reduces quality; 15 to 20 percent pose variation is acceptable (https://intercom.help/domoai/en/articles/12958755-character-to-video).
- Akool: inconsistent features with several faces, poor results at extreme angles (search summary of competitor and G2 posts, https://akool.com/pricing for plan limits).
- MiniMax H3 character-swap LoRAs (what H3 Studio is built on): drift with small faces, occlusion, fast motion, difficult angles, lighting changes, long clips; experimental; most prominent subject replaced unless named
  (https://huggingface.co/Alissonerdx/Minimax-H3-ComfyUI/blob/main/docs/character-swap.md).

## 3. Definition: what "beats Higgsfield" means (10 measurable targets)

Higgsfield publishes no accuracy numbers, so "beating" it cannot mean beating a published score. It means: on a fixed test set of clips, H3 Studio's automatic scorecard (section 4) passes at the
thresholds below, and the product behaviour is better on the axes where a local, open pipeline can be better. Thresholds marked (inferred) are starting points; calibrate them on 10 to 20 known-good and
known-bad renders before enforcing them (the incident output is the first known-bad sample).

| # | Target | How measured | Threshold | Higgsfield / competitor reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | A swap that did not happen is never shown as success | T1 not-a-copy test on every render | 0 silent copies; any failed T1 sets status `failed_swap` with the reason, never `done` | none documented by any competitor |
| G2 | Identity | median ArcFace cosine (output face vs reference face) | at least 0.45 median, at least 0.30 on the 10th percentile (inferred; buffalo_l w600k_r50) | no published number; Higgsfield reviews report drift on profiles |
| G3 | Output length equals input length | frame count, duration | exact frame count, duration error at most 1 frame, audio error at most 40 ms | Runway loops/trims; Kling can shorten; others not stated |
| G4 | Original audio kept | ffprobe + audio comparison | present, same duration, level within 1 dB, stream-copied when the codec allows | not stated by Higgsfield Recast or Genjutsu |
| G5 | Untouched background | PSNR and SSIM outside a dilated person region, compared with the source | PSNR at least 28 dB and SSIM at least 0.85 (inferred; a raw copy through the H3 VAE measured about 30 to 34 dB and 0.93 to 0.96 whole-frame) | Wan-Animate generates only inside the mask; Higgsfield Object Swap states it preserves the rest |
| G6 | Motion fidelity | face landmark trajectory error and flow correlation vs source | landmark error at most 0.15 face-widths mean, flow correlation at least 0.85 (inferred) | Kling, Act-Two, Wan-Animate all claim motion transfer; none publish a test |
| G7 | Temporal stability | consecutive-sample ArcFace cosine, frame-difference ratio vs source | consecutive cosine at least 0.80; temporal difference energy between 0.5x and 2x of the source (inferred) | VBench-style flicker and Video-Identity-Distance used in face-swap research (DreamID-V) |
| G8 | Clip length ceiling | longest clip with all tests passing | at least 60 s (4 segments) without a visible seam (colour jump at most 3 dE at joins) | Genjutsu 4 to 30 s, Kling 30 s, DomoAI 30 s, Viggle 1 min or more |
| G9 | Cost | marginal cost per output second | $0 fee; show GPU minutes per output second in the report | Genjutsu $0.37 to $1.05 per second |
| G10 | Honest speed | wall clock per output second, shown before and after the job | at most 60 s per output second on a free GPU with the DMD 8-step LoRA (inferred target; today 96 s measured on a shared GPU with the 4-step fallback) | Recast 30 s to 2 min per clip; Viggle seconds to under a minute |

Where H3 Studio will not beat Higgsfield without more work: raw speed (cloud GPUs), 1080p output, polish and presets. Say so in the UI instead of hiding it.

## 4. Rubric and automatic acceptance tests

Judging rubric users apply to swaps (from the sources in section 2): identity, motion fidelity, untouched background, lighting and skin-tone match, hands, occlusions, output length equal to input length,
original audio kept, speed. Research references for metrics: SSIM, LPIPS and FVD in Wan-Animate (SSIM 0.813, LPIPS 0.227, FVD 118.65 on its full benchmark, https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14055v1);
ArcFace cosine, consecutive-frame identity distance and VBench-style background / subject consistency and flicker in DreamID-V's IDBench-V (https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01425 , https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.11279);
SSIM 0.746, LPIPS 0.152, PSNR 23.09 reported by MoCha for whole-frame character replacement (search result summary; whole-frame numbers are low even for good swaps, which is why G5 uses the background only).

All tests run on CPU after the render, on sampled frames (for example 12 frames spread over the clip plus the first and last), with `providers=['CPUExecutionProvider']`. The tools exist already:
insightface `det_10g.onnx` (detection + 5 keypoints), `w600k_r50.onnx` (ArcFace embedding), cv2 (resize, optical flow, Lab colour), skimage SSIM, ffprobe/ffmpeg. No GPU, no ComfyUI call, no model download.
"Pre" tests run before the GPU job; "post" tests run on the result; each returns pass, warn or fail plus a number for the report.

| ID | Rubric item | When | Automatic test | Fail / warn rule | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T0 | Method preflight | pre | the swap LoRA the method was trained for is installed and selected; no silent fallback | fail (block start) if the official method has no `v1.1_ref2va_fro` or `v1.0_ref2va_r64_gl` LoRA and the user has not ticked "run degraded anyway" | root cause 2 of the incident |
| T0b | Prompt integrity | pre | official prompt equals the doc template outside `{details}`; `{details}` is at most about 300 characters, contains none of `<Video`, `<Picture`, "Replace the person", has no phantom `<Picture n>` beyond the number of references | fail (block start) | root cause 3; `check_graph.py` already diffs the template offline, run the same check on the live job |
| T0c | Source suitability | pre | detect the person on 5 source frames: face found on at least 80 percent, face width at least 80 px at the working resolution, one dominant person (second face area at most 50 percent of the first), no cut (histogram jump between consecutive sampled frames) | warn with the exact reason; Higgsfield's own tips are the list (one clear person, frontal, calm motion, no props in hand) | turns "tips" into a preflight, inferred benefit |
| T0d | Reference suitability | pre | face detected in the reference, one face, detection score at least 0.6, not a white background | warn or fail | Higgsfield: avoid pure white background |
| T1 | Not a copy of the source (the incident) | post | per sampled frame: ArcFace cosine of the main output face against (a) the source face and (b) the reference face. A copy has (a) high and (b) low | fail `failed_swap` if median cos(source, output) at least 0.60 AND median cos(reference, output) below 0.25; additionally fail if the whole-frame SSIM against the source is above 0.93 on at least 80 percent of sampled frames while the face check is inconclusive | measured on the incident output: cos(source, output) 0.77 to 0.98 (median about 0.93), cos(reference, output) -0.01 to 0.07, SSIM 0.93 to 0.96, PSNR 30 to 34 dB. Frames with no detected face give no vote |
| T2 | Identity | post | median and 10th percentile of cos(reference face, output face) over sampled frames with a detected face; fraction of frames where a face is found when the source had one | pass at median at least 0.45 and p10 at least 0.30; warn between 0.30 and 0.45; fail below 0.30 | (inferred) thresholds; use the reference's best view when it is a sheet (take the highest-scoring face crop) |
| T3 | Motion fidelity | post | compare normalised 5-keypoint trajectories (face centre, size, roll, eye-line) of the main face in source and output; plus Farneback or DIS optical-flow magnitude correlation inside the face-to-torso box | warn above 0.10 face-widths mean error, fail above 0.25; flow correlation below 0.70 warns | (inferred) values; the output should follow the source, since it is a guide |
| T4 | Untouched background | post | build a background mask on CPU: union of person boxes from the face detector scaled to a body box, dilated, or the SAM3 mask when the job produced one; PSNR and SSIM of the source vs output outside it | warn below 28 dB or 0.85, fail below 22 dB or 0.70 | the H3 VAE re-encode alone gives about 30 to 34 dB, so this is a regression guard, not a pixel-exactness claim |
| T5 | Lighting and skin tone | post | Lab mean of a cheek patch (from keypoints) in output vs reference; luminance of the output face vs the source face; warn if the delta E to the reference tone exceeds 15 or the face luminance differs from the source face by more than 25 percent | warn only | (inferred); catches the "pasted on" look that Wan-Animate's Relighting LoRA targets |
| T6 | Hands | post | no hand detector is installed in H3 Studio today. Option: DWPose onnx (yolox + dw-ll_ucoco) on CPU if the files are reachable read-only, else skipped | report "not tested" rather than pass | needs a decision on which pose model files to read |
| T7 | Occlusion and re-entry | post | sample the frames where the source face is hidden or tiny (no detection) and the next frames where it returns; require cos(reference, output) in the first 3 sampled frames after re-entry to be at least the 10th percentile of T2 | warn | Higgsfield: occluded face makes the swap fail |
| T8 | Temporal stability | post | consecutive-sample ArcFace cosine (video-identity-distance style); ratio of mean absolute frame difference output / source | warn below 0.80 consecutive cosine or outside 0.5x to 2x of the source energy | flicker, texture crawling (named in the official prompt) |
| T9 | Length equals input | post | ffprobe: frame count equals the plan from `GET /api/plan`, fps 24, resolution on the /32 grid, aspect ratio within 1 percent of the source | fail on any mismatch | exists partly in the server plan code; add it as an assertion on the file |
| T10 | Original audio kept | post | audio stream present when the source had one; duration within 40 ms of the video; mean volume (ffmpeg `astats`) within 1 dB of the source segment; if `voice != source`, only check presence | fail on missing or wrong length, warn on level | the server already cuts the original audio in one piece |
| T11 | Segment joins (more than 15 s) | post | colour step (Lab mean difference) between the last frame of part k and the first of part k+1, and ArcFace cosine across the join | warn above 3 dE or cosine below 0.80 | segments are independent H3 runs today |
| T12 | Speed | post | wall clock from job start to finish divided by output seconds; split queue wait and run time | report only; warn above the G10 target | job f0eaf24e: 1354 s / 14.04 s = 96 s per output second |

Job status model (proposal): `done` only when T0 to T2, T9 and T10 pass. T1 or T2 failing gives `failed_swap` (shown red, original never presented as the result, one click to retry with a new seed or open the compare panel).
Warnings give `done_with_warnings`, each warning with the number and the frame index that caused it. The report (JSON + a short text) is saved next to the output and rendered in the Result card.
A copy of the source must never reach the user as "Result".

Best-of-N: the existing Variations control (same setup, different seeds) can rank its results by T2 median plus T4, so the first thing the user sees is the best one (inferred benefit; no competitor documents this).

## 5. Gap list, ordered by impact

Impact is judged by how much the gap damages user trust or the result a user sees. Effort: S under half a day, M a day or two, L longer.

Status on 2026-11-19 (this table is the state of 2026-11-13): gaps 1, 2 and 3 are closed (`REPO/h3studio/verify.py`, the
guide-latent LoRA gate in `wf_h3swap.py`, the `{details}` checks in `h3_prompts.py`); for gap 12 the DMD 8-step LoRA is installed
and an ETA is shown (`eta.py`). The current ranked status of every gap is in `knowledge/roadmap.md`.

## 6. Implementation notes for the next agents

- Calibration facts measured on 2026-11-13, CPU only (insightface `det_10g` + `w600k_r50` through onnxruntime `CPUExecutionProvider`, 8 frames evenly spaced, output resized to the source size):
  incident output vs source, SSIM 0.934 to 0.964, PSNR 29.9 to 34.1 dB, cos(source, output) 0.765 to 0.980, one frame without a detected face, cos(reference, output) about -0.01 to 0.07
  (the reference was a very wide character sheet; the largest detected face was used, so the real reference score for a clean swap still needs a known-good render to confirm the 0.45 target).
  Script and numbers are in the analyst scratch directory under `agents/competitor-analyst/`.
- Resolve the reference embedding from the best view: run detection on the sheet, embed every face, keep the one whose average cosine to the others is highest.
- Never compare against a random frame: align by time (frame k of the output against frame k of the source, same size).
- Only a failing T1 should hard-block the "done" status; thresholds for T2 to T8 should start as warnings until calibrated on at least 10 good and 10 bad renders.
- Unresolved facts to verify before relying on them: Higgsfield Recast clip length and resolution (not on its page), Higgsfield audio policy (not stated), Runway Act-Two official limits (the help page blocked the fetch tool), Pika Pikaswaps limits (not on the FAQ), Viggle Remake length in the hosted product (only the open-weights figures are known), and Higgsfield's underlying model for Recast (one third-party page mentions "WAN 2.2 engine" in metadata only; treat as inferred).
